How it works

Set it once. The rest happens without you.

1

Set your threshold

Choose the stock level that counts as low for your store, and the address alerts should go to.

2

We check every day

Every product and variant in your store is checked once a day against that threshold.

3

You get one email

A single summary of what's running low, sorted with the most urgent items first.

Built to stay out of your way

No dashboards to check, no notifications to dismiss.

One email a day, never a flood

No repeat alerts

Questions

When does the daily email arrive?

Once a day, at the same time each day. If nothing is below your threshold, no email is sent.

Can I send alerts to someone else?

Yes. The alert address is whatever you enter in Settings, so it can go to a warehouse manager or a shared inbox rather than you.

Does it work with multiple locations?

Alerts are based on the total stock available for each variant across your store.

What data do you store?

Your store domain, your threshold, your alert email address, and a short record of which variants were recently alerted. No customer or order data is ever accessed. Everything is deleted when you uninstall.

How do I cancel?

Uninstall the app from your Shopify admin. Billing stops immediately and your data is deleted.
